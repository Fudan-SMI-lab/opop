HOW TO READ THIS FOR THE S7 DECISION:
  a low firing rate with 'no wall to act on' dominant  => the criterion, not the wiring, is
     the binding constraint, and S7 inherits 2e's coverage problem rather than fixing it
  a low rate with 'no undrawn value' dominant          => the mechanism is redundant with
     naive coverage: the sampler had already measured everything on the walled side
  a healthy rate with an early median first-firing     => there is room for the 12h pair to
     measure something, and P1-P4 become answerable
